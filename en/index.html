What you practise in Dardo

Short rounds of about 3 minutes. Dardo remembers what you find hard and asks it again.

  • LevelsLearn finishes step by step.
    Example: 121 is T20, T11, D14.121→T20·T11·D14
  • Segment blitzSegment values in seconds.
    Example: T17 is 51.T17→51
  • ScoringAdd up 3 darts in your head.
    Example: T20, 19 and T19 make 136.T20·19·T19→136
  • Match coach501 against opponents. The coach says what pros play.
    Example: 81 left with 2 darts, better T19.812 dartsBetter:T19(57)
  • CallerCall the throws and work out what is left.
    Example: T20, T20, 20 is 140, 361 left.T20·T20·20→140361 left
