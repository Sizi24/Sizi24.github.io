Common questions

How do I learn checkout routes?

Understand them instead of memorising: Dardo shows you the pro route with an explanation, you work it out yourself and practise it in levels. What you got wrong comes back later.

Is there a checkout calculator?

Yes, free and without the app: type your score into the checkout chart and you see the pro route at once. In Dardo you learn to work it out yourself.

Does Dardo replace practice at the board?

No. The board trains your throw, Dardo trains your head: maths, routes and decisions.

Which rules does it teach?

501 double out, as in tournaments and leagues. The caller also does 301.

Does Dardo collect data?

No. No account, no ads, no tracking, not on this site either.
